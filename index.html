A
STEP 01 • UNDERSTANDING

HOW FLOODS HAPPEN

Floods can occur when water accumulates faster than it can drain away. Heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers, poor drainage and excess water can cause large areas of land to become flooded.

B
STEP 02 • ASSAM

FLOODS IN ASSAM

Assam experiences recurring floods due to heavy monsoon rainfall, overflowing rivers and its geographical characteristics. The Brahmaputra and its tributaries play an important role in the region's flood conditions.

C
STEP 03 • IMPACT

EFFECTS OF FLOODS

Flooding can affect homes, roads, agriculture, animals, schools, infrastructure and the surrounding environment. Communities may also face displacement and disruption of daily life.

D
STEP 04 • SOLUTION

PREVENTION & PROTECTION

Flood risk can be reduced through proper drainage systems, protection of natural water bodies, better planning, early warning systems, awareness and suitable flood-control measures.
